# Math Mentors

**A peer-reviewed student math journal**

[math-mentors.org](https://math-mentors.org)

Math Mentors is a peer-reviewed math journal written by students. Authors learn LaTeX on their own, write their papers in the journal's template and review each other's work. Papers that pass peer review are published here in two issues each year.

Math Mentors publishes The Math Mentors Journal, a peer-reviewed journal of math papers written by high school students. Each paper is checked by two student reviewers before it is accepted, and every paper uses the same LaTeX template. Any topic in mathematics is welcome.

## Standards

**Complete proofs.** Two member reviewers check every proof before a paper is accepted. A computer check is treated as evidence and not as a proof, so the paper has to label it as a check.

**Citations.** Any result a paper uses without proving it needs a citation. Surveys have to cite the original papers and books instead of relying on Wikipedia or blogs.

**Known results and original work.** Each paper has to say which parts are known results and which parts are the author's own work. Expository papers are accepted, so a paper does not need a new result to be published.

## Joining

New members learn LaTeX on their own first and then move on to writing and reviewing papers, so no LaTeX experience is needed to join. To join or ask a question, email contact@math-mentors.org. The [submission guidelines](https://math-mentors.org/submit) explain how to send a paper.
